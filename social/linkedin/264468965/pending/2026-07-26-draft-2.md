Those locked into ONE AI vendor. And those who can move.

I'm watching smart CTOs make a critical mistake right now.

They sign enterprise deals with a single model provider. Exclusive infrastructure. Proprietary agent frameworks. Deep integration.

Feels safe. Feels smart.

Until a better model drops. Or pricing shifts. Or you need to run private models for compliance.

Suddenly you're not choosing the best tool for the job. You're negotiating contract terms.

The winners building NOW are using orchestration layers that swap models like API calls. Open-source frameworks. Multi-cloud deployment.

Your AI stack should be a decision you make WEEKLY, not a contract you signed YEARLY.

Who owns your AI architecture — you or your vendor?
