Most people drag into Monday like it's punishment.

Complaining about the weekend ending. Counting days until Friday. Treating work like something to survive.

That mindset is why they stay stuck.

Monday is fresh momentum. A full week of opportunity. A blank canvas for the work that matters.

The people building real things? They don't dread Monday. They attack it.

They've been thinking about that problem since Saturday. That feature. That conversation. That opportunity.

Monday isn't the enemy. Monday is the starting line.

You want different results? Stop treating the week like something to get through. Start treating it like something to own.

What's the ONE thing you're committing to ship this week?
