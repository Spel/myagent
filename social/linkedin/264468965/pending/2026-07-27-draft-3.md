Not always. Not forever. But right now? Absolutely.

Large enterprises have budget, talent, and infrastructure. They also have:

- 6-month procurement cycles
- 47 approval layers
- Legacy systems that break if you sneeze
- Committees that debate while markets shift

Small teams? They decide on Tuesday and ship on Friday.

I've watched 8-person companies outpace 800-person companies in AI deployment. Not because they're smarter. Because they're faster.

The window is open. Speed beats scale. Execution beats planning.

This won't last forever. The giants will figure it out eventually.

But today? Being small is an unfair advantage.

Are you using it or wasting it?
