𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗿𝗶𝗰𝗵𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗳𝗲𝗲𝗱𝗯𝗮𝗰𝗸 𝗹𝗼𝗼𝗽 𝗶𝘀 𝘀𝗺𝗮𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗿 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝗻 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸.

You don't need 10,000 customers to know if you're on track.

You need 10.

Ten people who tell you the truth. Ten who complain when something's broken. Ten who stick around when you fix it.

Most founders chase scale before signal. They want the vanity metrics before the product-market fit.

But the real data? It's in the small conversations. The DMs. The churn you can explain. The repeat purchases that surprise you.

Stop looking for the big number. Start listening to the small ones.

Who are your 10?
