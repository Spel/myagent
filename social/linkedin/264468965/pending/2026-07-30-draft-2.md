𝗠𝗼𝘀𝘁 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁𝘂𝗽𝘀 𝗱𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝗱𝗶𝗲 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻. 𝗧𝗵𝗲𝘆 𝗱𝗶𝗲 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝗶𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻.

You know what kills momentum faster than a bad product?

Seven meetings about one decision.

A roadmap that changes every week because someone read a blog post.

Features built for the one customer who might churn instead of the ten who stay.

Speed isn't about moving fast. It's about deciding fast.

The wrong decision made today beats the perfect decision made next month.

Because you can course-correct. You can't course-correct a decision you never made.

What's one thing you've been overthinking? Decide. Ship. Move on.
