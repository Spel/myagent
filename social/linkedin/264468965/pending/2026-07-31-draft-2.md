𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗰𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗿 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗼𝗻𝗲 𝘄𝗵𝗼 𝗽𝗮𝘆𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗺𝗼𝘀𝘁.

It's the one who tells you when something's broken.

The quiet ones? They just leave.

The ones who complain? They're giving you a chance to fix it. That's loyalty.

Most teams treat complaints as problems. They shouldn't. Complaints are data. Free product feedback from someone who still cares enough to speak up.

The real danger sign isn't the angry email. It's the silence after a long period of engagement.

Churn doesn't announce itself. It just stops showing up.

Who's your most vocal complainer? Go thank them today.
