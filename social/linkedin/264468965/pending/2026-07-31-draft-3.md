It's a stack of daily choices.

You don't wake up one day and suddenly have a built brand. Or a shipped product. Or a team that runs without you.

You get there by showing up every day. Making one decision. Taking one action. Writing one post. Having one conversation.

The gap feels huge. It's not. It's just a lot of small steps disguised as a mountain.

Stop looking at the distance. Start counting the steps.

What's one small step you can take today?
