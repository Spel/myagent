Single-agent AI was step one. One model. One task. One output.

Multi-agent is step two. One agent researches. Another validates. A third writes. A fourth checks for compliance.

They talk to each other. They catch mistakes. They ship work that used to require a team.

The shift isn't about intelligence. It's about coordination.

The teams building agent orchestration layers right now? They're not just automating tasks. They're automating workflows. Entire processes.

This is the layer where 10x engineers become 100x teams.

What would your org look like with 10 agents instead of 10 tools?
