𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗳𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗱𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝗮𝗹𝗹 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗮𝗻𝘀𝘄𝗲𝗿𝘀. 𝗧𝗵𝗲𝘆 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗾𝘂𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀.

The ones who ask: "What's the one thing blocking us this week?"

Not: "How do we build a billion-dollar company?" That's too big. Too abstract.

Great founders debug their business like engineers debug code. One bottleneck at a time.

Shipping slow? Find the blocker.
Conversion low? Find the friction.
Team overwhelmed? Find the missing system.

Big visions are fine. But they don't ship. Questions ship.

What's the ONE question you should be asking about your business right now?
