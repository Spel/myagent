Everyone slows down in August. Vacations. Half-days. "We'll pick it up in September."

That's exactly why you shouldn't.

While everyone else is checking out, you can be checking in.

One extra post. One extra call. One extra feature shipped while the competition is on the beach.

September doesn't care about August's excuses. It shows up ready.

The question isn't "Should I rest?" Rest is good. Rest is necessary.

The question is: "Am I resting to recover, or resting to avoid?"

What's one thing you can ship this month while everyone else is slowing down?
