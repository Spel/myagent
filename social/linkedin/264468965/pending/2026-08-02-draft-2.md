Skills can be taught. Curiosity can't.

The person who asks "Why do we do it this way?" is worth more than the person who just executes.

The one who says "What if we tried this instead?" is the one who finds the 10x improvement.

Most hiring optimizes for the wrong signal. Years of experience. Brand names. Degrees.

But the team that wins? They hire for hunger. For questions. For people who aren't satisfied with "because that's how we've always done it."

What's the best question a candidate ever asked you?
