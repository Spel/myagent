Most people spend Sunday evening in dread mode. Preparing for Monday. Answering emails they ignored all weekend. Building anxiety about the week ahead.

What if Sunday was for strategy instead?

One hour. No screens. Just a notebook and your brain.

What matters this week? What doesn't? What's the ONE thing you want to ship?

That clarity is worth more than 47 email replies.

Monday doesn't have to hit you like a wave. You can walk into it with a plan.

What would change if you spent one hour thinking instead of reacting?

#Mindset #SundayStrategy #PlanYourWeek #ThinkBeforeYouReact
