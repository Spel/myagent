Everyone's learning prompt engineering. That's table stakes.

The real edge? Knowing how to wire agents together.

One agent that researches. Another that validates. A third that formats. A fourth that routes output to the right place.

It's not about building the smartest agent. It's about building the right chain.

Most teams are still thinking in single-model terms. "Which LLM should we use?"

The teams pulling ahead are thinking in workflows. "How do we connect these 5 agents to solve this entire problem end-to-end?"

Prompt engineering is the easy part. The hard part is architecture.

What's one workflow you could turn into an agent chain this month?
