𝗬𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗹𝗮𝘇𝘆. 𝗬𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗱𝗶𝘀𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗻𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗳𝗿𝗼𝗺 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗼𝘄𝗻 𝘄𝗵𝘆.

When you don't post, it's not because you're lazy.

When you don't ship, it's not because you're undisciplined.

It's because you've lost the thread between what you're doing and why you started.

The work feels heavy because the connection is broken.

The fix isn't more willpower. It's reconnection.

Why did you start this? Who are you trying to help? What's the version of you that you're building toward?

Laziness is a symptom. Disconnection is the cause.

What's the ONE reason you started this journey?

#Mindset #Reconnect #YourWhy #RememberWhyYouStarted
