Not because you've automated it. Because the customer already decided.

They read your content. Watched your demo. Talked to your users. Checked your reviews.

By the time they reach out, they're 80% sold. The call is just confirmation.

Most teams optimize for more calls. More pitches. More volume.

The better play? Optimize for fewer calls with higher intent.

Build the presence that does the selling before you show up. The content that answers objections you haven't heard yet. The proof that makes promises credible.

How many of your sales calls are really just first dates?
