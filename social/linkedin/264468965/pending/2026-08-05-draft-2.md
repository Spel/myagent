𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 "𝘆𝗲𝘀" 𝗰𝗼𝘀𝘁𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘀𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴. 𝗘𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 "𝗻𝗼" 𝘀𝗮𝘃𝗲𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝘀𝗼𝗺𝗲𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴.

Most founders say yes too often.

Yes to every meeting. Every feature request. Every partnership. Every opportunity that sounds good on paper.

But every yes has a hidden cost: Focus. Time. Energy. The ability to go deep on what matters.

The best founders aren't the ones who do more. They're the ones who say no to almost everything.

No to good opportunities so they can say yes to great ones.

No to distraction so they can say yes to depth.

What's one "yes" you need to turn into a "no" this week?

#StartupLife #Focus #SayNo #DepthOverBreadth
