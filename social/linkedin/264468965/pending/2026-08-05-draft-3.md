𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗵𝗮𝗿𝗱𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗽𝗮𝗿𝘁 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴. 𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿.

The first time feels risky. But you have nothing to lose.

Starting over? That's different. Now you have something to protect. A reputation. A routine. A version of success that's comfortable but not right.

The people who grow the most aren't the ones who start once. They're the ones willing to start over repeatedly.

Each restart is a chance to apply what you learned. To correct the path. To align with who you've become, not who you were.

The real trap isn't failure. It's staying stuck in a success that no longer fits.

What would you start over if you weren't afraid of losing what you have?
