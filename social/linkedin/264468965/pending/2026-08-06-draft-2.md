𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝘁𝗼 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝗲 𝗮 𝗰𝘂𝘀𝘁𝗼𝗺𝗲𝗿 𝘄𝗮𝘀 𝘀𝗶𝘅 𝗺𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗵𝘀 𝗮𝗴𝗼. 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝘀𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗱 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲 𝗶𝘀 𝗻𝗼𝘄.

Some customers drain more than they pay.

They demand endless customization. Require constant hand-holding. Negotiate every invoice. Never refer others.

The revenue looks good on paper. The reality? They're costing you the customers who matter.

Every hour spent on the wrong customer is an hour not spent on the right one.

Firing a customer isn't failure. It's focus.

Who's the one customer you've been afraid to let go?
