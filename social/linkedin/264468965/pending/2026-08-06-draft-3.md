𝗬𝗼𝘂 𝗱𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱 𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲 𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲. 𝗬𝗼𝘂 𝗻𝗲𝗲𝗱 𝗳𝗲𝘄𝗲𝗿 𝗱𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀.

Everyone says they're busy. Most people are just distracted.

The notifications. The open tabs. The "quick checks" that become 45 minutes.

Time isn't the constraint. Attention is.

You have enough hours. You don't have enough protected focus.

The people who ship aren't the ones with more time. They're the ones who block time for what matters and protect it like their life depends on it.

What's one distraction you could eliminate today to buy back an hour of focus?
