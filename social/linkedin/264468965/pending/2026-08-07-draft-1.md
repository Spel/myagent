You don't write features anymore. You describe them.

You don't debug. You explain the problem.

You don't architect. You negotiate with agents who propose solutions.

The skill shift is massive. From syntax to semantics. From implementation to intent.

The engineers who thrive will be the ones who can articulate what they want clearly enough that an agent can build it.

Code is becoming the assembly language of thought. The real programming happens in natural language.

What would you build if you could just describe it and have it exist?
