Idea quality matters. But speed matters more.

The founder who ships a B+ idea in 2 weeks beats the founder who ships an A+ idea in 6 months.

Why? Because the market doesn't wait. The B+ idea is already learning from real users. The A+ idea is still theoretical.

Perfect is the enemy of shipped. Every time.

The best founders I know aren't geniuses. They're just relentless about getting things out the door.

What's one thing you've been overthinking that you could ship this week?
