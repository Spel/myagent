𝗬𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗯𝗲𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗱. 𝗬𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗼𝗻 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗼𝘄𝗻 𝘁𝗶𝗺𝗲𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗲.

Comparison is the thief of more than joy. It's the thief of progress.

You see someone else's success and think: "I should be there by now."

But their timeline isn't your timeline. Their starting point wasn't your starting point. Their resources, network, luck, timing — all different.

The only fair comparison is you today vs. you yesterday.

Are you moving forward? Are you learning? Are you building something that matters to you?

If yes, you're not behind. You're exactly where you need to be.

What would change if you stopped comparing and started tracking your own progress?
