𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗶𝘁𝗼𝗿𝘀 𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗻'𝘁 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗯𝗶𝗴𝗴𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗿𝗲𝗮𝘁. 𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗼𝘄𝗻 𝗶𝗻𝗲𝗿𝘁𝗶𝗮 𝗶𝘀.

Competitors you can see. You can study their moves. Prepare responses.

Inertia is invisible. It's the "we've always done it this way" that feels like wisdom but is actually decay.

It's the process that made sense 2 years ago but doesn't anymore. The feature that 3 customers use but you can't kill. The meeting that no longer serves a purpose but still shows up on calendars.

The best founders don't just watch competitors. They audit their own defaults.

What's one thing you're doing today that you'd never start if you were building from scratch?
