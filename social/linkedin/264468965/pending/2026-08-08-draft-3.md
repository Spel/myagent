𝗦𝘂𝗰𝗰𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝗮 𝗱𝗲𝘀𝘁𝗶𝗻𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻. 𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝗮 𝗯𝘆𝗽𝗿𝗼𝗱𝘂𝗰𝘁 𝗼𝗳 𝘄𝗵𝗼 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗯𝗲𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗲.

Chase success directly and it runs.

Chase growth, learning, contribution — and success shows up as a side effect.

The people who build something meaningful aren't focused on the outcome. They're focused on becoming the kind of person who naturally produces that outcome.

Skill by skill. Day by day. Decision by decision.

The goal isn't to be successful. The goal is to be undeniable.

What would you do differently if you stopped chasing success and started chasing who you need to become?
