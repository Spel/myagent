Most teams are waiting for the perfect agent architecture. The right framework. The proven pattern.

They'll wait forever.

The teams learning the most right now are the ones who shipped something that broke. Who deployed an agent that hallucinated. Who built a chain that failed on edge cases.

Each failure taught them something. Each broken workflow revealed what actually matters.

The cost of building wrong is low. The cost of not building is infinite.

What's the simplest agent you could ship this week — even if it breaks?
