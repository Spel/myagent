𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗯𝗿𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝘆𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗹𝗼𝗴𝗼. 𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝘄𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗽𝗲𝗼𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝘀𝗮𝘆 𝘄𝗵𝗲𝗻 𝘆𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗶𝗻 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗿𝗼𝗼𝗺.

You can spend millions on brand strategy. Design the perfect logo. Craft the perfect messaging.

But your brand is built in conversations you never hear.

The way your customer describes you to a friend. The reason a prospect chose your competitor. The thing former employees say about working there.

That's your brand. Everything else is just decoration.

The best founders don't just manage the brand they show. They manage the brand that spreads.

What do you think people say about your company when you're not in the room?
