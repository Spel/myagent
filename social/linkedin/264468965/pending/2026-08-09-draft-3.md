𝗦𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗮𝘆 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘂𝗽. 𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗸𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗮𝗵𝗲𝗮𝗱.

Most people spend Sunday in reactive mode. Answering emails. Finishing tasks. Preparing for Monday.

The people who win spend Sunday in strategic mode.

What matters this week? What doesn't? What's the one move that changes everything?

One hour of thinking beats ten hours of reacting. But you have to protect that hour.

The week doesn't care about your good intentions. It cares about your priorities.

What's the one thing you want to be true by next Sunday?

#Mindset #SundayStrategy #ThinkAhead #OneThingThatMatters
