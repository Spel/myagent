𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗱𝗲𝗰𝗶𝘀𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘌 𝗮𝗿𝗲𝗻'𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗼𝗻𝗲𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗺𝗮𝗸𝗲. 𝗧𝗵𝗲𝘆'𝗿𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗼𝗻𝗲𝘀 𝘆𝗼𝘂 𝗱𝗼𝗻'𝘁 𝗵𝗮𝘃𝗲 𝘁𝗼 𝗺𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗮𝗻𝘆𝗺𝗼𝗿𝗲.

Every decision you make is a tax on your focus.

The best founders don't optimize for making better decisions. They optimize for eliminating decisions.

Uniforms. Meal plans. Meeting cadences. Standardized processes.

Not because they're rigid. Because they're protecting their decision budget for what actually matters.

You have maybe 3-5 high-quality decisions in you per day. Everything else should be on autopilot.

What's one decision you're still making that you could automate or eliminate?

#StartupLife #DecisionFatigue #EliminateDecisions #ProtectYourFocus
