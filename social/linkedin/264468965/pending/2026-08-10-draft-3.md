𝗠𝗼𝗻𝗱𝗮𝘆 𝗶𝘀𝗻'𝘁 𝗮 𝗳𝗿𝗲𝘀𝗵 𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗿𝘁. 𝗜𝘁'𝘀 𝗮 𝗰𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗻𝘂𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.

The people who dread Monday are the ones who disconnected from their purpose on Friday.

They spent the weekend avoiding. Now Monday is here to collect.

The people who attack Monday? They never fully left. Their mind was always working on the problem. The weekend wasn't escape — it was incubation.

Monday isn't the enemy. Disconnection is.

The best way to love Monday is to stay connected to what you're building. Not obsessively. But intentionally.

What's the one thing you're building that makes Monday worth showing up for?
