"This would be cool to build with AI" is how you build products nobody wants.

"I've had this problem for 3 years and nothing solves it" is how you build products people pay for.

The technology isn't the starting point. The pain is.

The best AI products I've seen started with founders who were obsessed with a problem. AI was just the solution that finally made it solvable.

Don't start with "What can AI do?" Start with "What's broken that AI could fix?"

What's one problem you've stopped trying to solve because it seemed impossible?
