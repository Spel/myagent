𝗬𝗼𝘂𝗿 𝗯𝗶𝗴𝗴𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗲𝘁𝗶𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲 𝗮𝗱𝘃𝗮𝗻𝘁𝗮𝗴𝗲 𝗶𝘀 𝘄𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝘆𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝘄𝗶𝗹𝗹𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗼 𝘀𝘂𝗳𝗳𝗲𝗿 𝘁𝗵𝗿𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵.

Anyone can copy your product. Your pricing. Your marketing.

What they can't copy is what you're willing to endure.

The late nights. The difficult conversations. The decisions that hurt in the short term but are right for the long term.

The competitors who seem to be winning? They're optimizing for comfort. You're optimizing for something else.

That gap — between what you'll suffer and what they'll suffer — is your moat.

What's the one thing you're willing to do that your competitors won't?
