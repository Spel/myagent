This is the future most companies aren't ready for.

Everyone's excited about AI agents — autonomous systems that can reason, plan, and execute. But what happens when multiple agents want the same thing? When they have conflicting goals? When there's no clear hierarchy?

We're not just building tools anymore. We're building digital workers with their own priorities. And just like human teams, they'll have friction, politics, and competition.

The companies that figure out agent orchestration — how to coordinate, prioritize, and resolve conflicts — will have the real advantage.

The future isn't one AI. It's many. And they need management too.
