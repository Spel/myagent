Talks are heating up. Stripe + Advent are circling.

Think about that for a second. PayPal — the company that defined online payments — might become just another acquisition.

This isn't about PayPal. It's about what happens when you stop moving.

Stripe didn't exist when PayPal was already a household name. But Stripe kept shipping. Kept iterating. Kept making payments invisible.

PayPal? They rested. They became the thing you use because you have to, not because you want to.

The lesson: market leadership isn't a title you earn once. It's a position you defend every single day by staying faster, simpler, more useful than the alternative.

When you stop shipping, someone else starts eating.

What have you shipped this month that makes you unreplaceable?
