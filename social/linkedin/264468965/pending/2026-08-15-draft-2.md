A few weeks ago, Anthropic announced watermarks to catch AI use. Now Google is going the opposite direction — making AI outputs feel more "yours."

Two different philosophies. One clear signal: the industry hasn't figured out transparency yet.

Watermarks solve for attribution. Removing them solves for ownership. Both feel right depending on where you sit.

But here's what matters for builders: the user experience wins. Every time.

Users don't want to explain "I used AI." They want results they can call their own. The platforms that reduce friction — not add it — will win the adoption war.

Transparency is important. But so is empowerment. The winning platforms will figure out how to do both without making users feel like they're filing paperwork.

How does your product balance trust with usability?
