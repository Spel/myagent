𝗔𝗽𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝘄𝗮𝗻𝘁𝘀 𝟭𝟱% 𝗼𝗳 𝗽𝘂𝗿𝗰𝗵𝗮𝘀𝗲𝘀 𝗺𝗮𝗱𝗲 𝗼𝘂𝘁𝘀𝗶𝗱𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗔𝗽𝗽 𝗦𝘁𝗼𝗿𝗲.

Not through the App Store. Outside of it.

They're proposing a 15% cut on purchases users make via links — even when those links lead to your own website.

Let that sink in. Apple wants a commission on traffic they didn't drive, infrastructure they didn't provide, and transactions they didn't process.

This isn't about App Store fees. This is about platform power.

When you build on someone else's land, they can change the rent anytime. The rules. The access. The terms.

The lesson for founders: every platform dependency is a strategic risk. Build on them. Grow on them. But always have an off-ramp.

What platform are you too dependent on right now?
