𝗔𝗻𝘁𝗵𝗿𝗼𝗽𝗶𝗰'𝘀 𝗖𝗘𝗢 𝗰𝗮𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗔𝗜 𝗯𝗮𝗰𝗸𝗹𝗮𝘀𝗵 "𝗮 𝗰𝗿𝗶𝘀𝗶𝘀 𝗼𝗳 𝘁𝗿𝘂𝘀𝘁."

Not a crisis of technology. Not a crisis of regulation. A crisis of trust.

He's right. The biggest barrier to AI adoption isn't capability — it's credibility.

People don't trust what they don't understand. They don't trust what they can't control. They don't trust what feels like it's happening to them rather than with them.

Every AI company is racing to build smarter models. The winners will be the ones who build trust faster.

Transparency. Explainability. User control. These aren't features — they're the product. Without trust, capability is meaningless.

The question for builders: are you optimizing for what your AI can do, or what your users can trust?
