Meta released Glimmer — an open-weight AI model. Zuckerberg wrote a letter about AI being "for everyone." He's positioning Meta as the open alternative to closed labs.

But the market isn't convinced. The narrative isn't landing.

Why? Because trust isn't built through press releases. It's built through track record.

When you've spent years monetizing user data, optimizing for engagement over wellbeing, and treating privacy as an obstacle — one open model doesn't rewrite the story.

The lesson: your brand is the sum of every decision you've made. You can't message your way into trust. You earn it through consistent action over time.

Open-weight models are great. But openness is more than code. It's how you've treated the people you're now asking to trust you.

What does your track record say about you?
