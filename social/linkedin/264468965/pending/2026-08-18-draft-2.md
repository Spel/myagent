𝗬𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗻𝗼𝘁 𝗯𝘂𝘀𝘆. 𝗬𝗼𝘂'𝗿𝗲 𝗱𝗶𝘀𝘁𝗿𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗯𝘆 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘄𝗿𝗼𝗻𝗴 𝘁𝗵𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀.

Every founder says they don't have time. But track where the hours actually go:

- Responding to emails that could wait
- In meetings that should have been memos
- Optimizing things that don't move the needle
- Doom-scrolling competitor updates

The real constraint isn't time. It's clarity.

The founders who move fast aren't working more hours. They're saying no to more things. They've decided what matters and everything else is noise.

Here's the test: If you had 2 hours today with zero interruptions, would you know exactly what to work on?

If the answer is "it depends" — you don't have a time problem. You have a priorities problem.

What would you do with those 2 hours?
