Two years ago, everyone was panicking about AI taking jobs.

Now the picture is clearer: AI didn't eliminate work. It eliminated the lower half of the skill curve.

The bar for "good enough" is now higher. The average is free. If you're not bringing judgment, taste, strategy, or relationships — you're competing with a machine that works 24/7 for pennies.

But here's the flip side: the ceiling is also higher. AI amplifies what you're already good at. If you have taste, you can produce more. If you have strategy, you can test faster. If you have relationships, you can serve better.

The winners aren't fighting AI. They're using it to 10x their edge.

What's your edge — and how are you amplifying it?
