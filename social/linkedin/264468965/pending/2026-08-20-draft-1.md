𝗔𝗜 𝘄𝗮𝘀 𝘀𝘂𝗽𝗽𝗼𝘀𝗲𝗱 𝘁𝗼 𝘄𝗶𝗻 𝗽𝗲𝗼𝗽𝗹𝗲 𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝗯𝘆 𝗻𝗼𝘄. 𝗜𝘁 𝗵𝗮𝘀𝗻'𝘁.

Every prediction said AI would be indispensable by now. Everywhere. Used by everyone. A fundamental shift in how we work and live.

The reality? Adoption is slower than expected. Trust is lower than predicted. And the average person still can't tell you what AI actually does for them.

The tech industry built incredible capabilities. But capabilities don't equal adoption. Adoption requires utility, trust, and a reason to change existing habits.

We're in the gap between "can" and "does." AI can do amazing things. But for most people, it doesn't — yet.

The winners of the AI era won't be the ones with the best models. They'll be the ones who close this gap. Who make AI useful enough, simple enough, trustworthy enough that people actually change how they live and work.

The technology is here. The user experience isn't. That's the opportunity.
