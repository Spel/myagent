A new study just quantified what everyone suspected: the internet is being rewritten by machines.

One in three pages. That's not a gradual shift. That's a structural change in how content gets made.

The implications cut both ways:

On one hand — democratization. More people can produce content. Barriers to entry have collapsed. The playing field is wider than ever.

On the other — saturation. The average quality of content has dropped. Noise is up. The bar for standing out just got higher.

The lesson: when everyone has the same tool, the advantage shifts from access to application. AI can write. But it can't replace your perspective, your experience, your voice.

The winners in the AI content era aren't the ones using AI the most. They're the ones using it to amplify what's already unique about them.
