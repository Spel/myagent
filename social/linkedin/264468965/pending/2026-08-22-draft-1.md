For two years, the story was about models. GPT-4. Claude. Gemini. The race for the smartest AI.

Nvidia just shifted the narrative. Their latest move proves something the best founders already knew: the infrastructure around a product often matters more than the product itself.

The model is the engine. But the harness — the tooling, the deployment, the safety rails, the integration layer — that's what makes it actually useful in production.

Sound familiar? It's the exact same pattern in every technology shift:

- The OS mattered more than the hardware
- The app store mattered more than the phone
- The API mattered more than the algorithm

The lesson: don't just build the thing. Build what makes the thing work. The scaffolding is often more valuable than the building.

What harness are you building around your core product?
