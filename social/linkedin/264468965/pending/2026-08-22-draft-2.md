Tesla announced the solar roof in 2016. It was beautiful. Revolutionary. The future of home energy.

A decade later? It's over.

The problem wasn't the vision. Solar roofs are a genuinely good idea. The problem was everything else: manufacturing complexity, installation costs, supply chain issues, and a business model that couldn't scale.

This is the founder trap: falling in love with the idea and underestimating the execution.

Elon Musk has pulled off impossible things. But even he couldn't make the economics work. Sometimes the market tells you "no" even when you're right about the vision.

The lesson: a great idea with bad execution is just a bad business. Execution isn't the boring part. It's the whole game.

What "impossible" idea in your business needs a reality check on execution?
