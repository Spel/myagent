• One person telling another
• A post that made them stop scrolling
• A conversation at an event
• A cold email you sent personally
• A problem you solved publicly

The founders who try to buy their first 100 customers? They burn cash and learn nothing.

The founders who earn their first 100 customers? They build something that doesn't need ads to grow.
